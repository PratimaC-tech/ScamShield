Student Recruitment Safety Guide

Learn common warning signs before you encounter them.

💰

Requests for money

Be cautious when an employer asks you to pay a registration, processing, training, security or other fee before joining.

⏰

Pressure to act fast

Urgent deadlines can make it harder to independently verify a recruiter, company or link.

🔐

Sensitive information

Do not casually provide passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, banking credentials or unnecessary identity information.

🔗

Suspicious links

Check the domain carefully. A link that looks similar to a company name is not automatically an official company website.

🎯

Unrealistic promises

Guaranteed jobs, guaranteed placements or unusually easy income claims deserve extra scrutiny.

📱

Unusual communication

Be cautious when recruitment depends entirely on informal channels or when someone asks for credentials.

When in doubt, verify first.
