THE PROBLEM

Fake opportunities can look completely real.

Recruitment scams can use pressure, payment requests, suspicious links and requests for sensitive information.

💰

Payment Requests

Registration, processing, training or security fees can be a major warning sign.

⏰

Urgency

Pressure to act immediately can reduce the time available for independent verification.

🔐

Sensitive Data

Unverified recruiters should not receive passwords, OTPs, banking credentials or similar sensitive information.
